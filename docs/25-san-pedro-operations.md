# San Pedro pilot operating guide

San Pedro staff have agreed to operate verification and complaints. Use assigned staff accounts and approved contact channels for pilot work. Assign a primary and a backup operator before the handover rehearsal.

## Verification and complaints

1. Review pending verification in the staff dashboard and record the outcome in the app. Handle resident identity documents only through the assigned account and agreed process.
2. Open **Reports** regularly on the staff schedule. Move a complaint to **Reviewing**, inspect the complaint and related evidence, and record what was checked in the private notes. Open a reported conversation only when it is relevant to the complaint.
3. Set **Resolved** or **Dismissed** after deciding the outcome. Write a separate message explaining the decision and any next step to the resident. Hide or restore a reported review only when the relevant moderation decision calls for it, and record the rationale.
4. For a stalled or disputed cancellation, use the complaint process to contact the parties through the agreed channel. The hired participants control the app cancellation decision. Escalate unresolved cases through the designated barangay contact. Agree and publish that contact before pilot acceptance.

## Handover checklist

| Responsibility | Handover item |
| --- | --- |
| San Pedro verification and reports | Name the primary and backup staff members. Rehearse verification, complaint intake, review evidence, and the resident-facing outcome with synthetic cases. |
| Technical custody | Name the technical custodian and backup. Verify account access and recovery for GitHub, Supabase, Vercel, the demo domain, and the Android signing account. |
| Support and escalation | Record the resident support contact, staff review schedule, escalation contact, and what to do when the app or account is unavailable. |
| Backups and recovery | Assign ownership and cadence for database and Storage backups. Rehearse a private database restore and Storage recovery. Keep the recovery record with the release process. |
| Hosting and continuation | Record the hosting owner, funding source, and maintenance agreement when the stakeholders confirm them. |

See the [release workflow](19-release-workflow.md) for verified backup and deployment steps and the [panel contract](24-panel-updates.md) for cancellation behavior.
